Privacy policy

How this website handles information. Last updated: October 2026.

← Back to home

In short

This site does not have user accounts or comment boxes, and its own pages do not collect personal information. The one exception is our article submission form, which is a Google Form: if you choose to use it, the details you enter go to us through Google.

What runs in your browser

The quiz, scenarios and article search run entirely in your browser. Your answers and searches are not sent to us or stored. Small notes, such as whether you have closed the welcome message or when your visit was last counted, are kept in your browser’s local storage on your device.

Third-party services

  • GitHub Pages hosts this site. GitHub may record technical data such as IP addresses in its server logs under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Fonts loads the site’s typefaces. Your browser requests the font files from Google, which may receive your IP address.
  • Google Analytics measures how the site is used. It sets cookies and receives technical data such as your IP address, browser, device and the pages you view, and processes them under Google’s own privacy policy.
  • Google Forms hosts our article submission form. Google processes the answers you submit under its own privacy policy. We use them only to review and publish submissions and to contact you about them.
  • LinkedIn, Instagram and WhatsApp links take you to those services, which have their own privacy practices. If you join a WhatsApp channel or community, WhatsApp’s rules apply, and other members or admins may see your name or number depending on your settings.

Cookies and analytics

This site uses Google Analytics to understand, in aggregate, which pages are visited and how people find the site. Google Analytics sets cookies in your browser and receives technical data such as your IP address, browser and device type, approximate location and the pages you view. Google processes this under its own privacy policy. We use the reports only to improve the site and do not use them to identify individual visitors. You can block or delete these cookies in your browser settings, or use Google’s opt-out browser add-on. Apart from Google Analytics, the site does not set its own cookies.

Children

The site is intended for a general audience and is not directed at children under 13.

Your rights

Because we do not collect personal information directly, there is generally nothing to access or delete. If you have questions, contact us through the contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date above shows the latest version.
