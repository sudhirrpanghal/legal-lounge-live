# Publishing content (no HTML editing)

1. Open `https://legallounge.live/admin.html` and sign in with Microsoft.
2. Open **Add new content**, pick the type (article, research paper, job, internship), fill the form.
3. Click **Save as draft** to review later, or **Publish now** to put it on the website straight away.

Where it appears:
- Articles: the "Latest articles" section on the Articles page.
- Research papers: the "Published research papers" section on the Research Papers page.
- Jobs and internships: "Latest approved opportunities" on the Careers page.
- Each item has its own page at `read.html?id=…` with the full text and Apply / PDF buttons.

Managing items (Drafts, Published, Pending, Rejected filters):
- **Edit** any item to fix typos (changes show on the website within about a minute).
- **Unpublish** takes an item off the website and keeps it as a draft.
- **Delete** removes a draft or rejected item permanently.
- Visitor submissions arrive as **Pending**: Approve, Reject, or Edit first and then Approve.

Writing tips: a blank line starts a new paragraph. Use `## Heading`, `- bullet`, `**bold**` and `[text](https://link)`.
Jobs and internships disappear from the Careers page automatically after their "Apply by" date.
