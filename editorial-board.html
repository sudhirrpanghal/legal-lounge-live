Editorial Governance Structure

EDITOR-IN-CHIEF

Office of Editorial Direction

Oversees editorial policy, legal accuracy, compliance with statutory updates (including BNS, BNSS, BSA 2023), and final approval for published research papers and landmark case briefs.

ACADEMIC & PEER-REVIEW PANEL

Senior Advisory Board

Comprising practicing advocates, judicial law clerks, and law academicians reviewing manuscripts on Constitutional Law, Criminal Jurisprudence, and Corporate Commercial laws.

STUDENT EDITORIAL COMMITTEE

Editorial Fellows & Fact-Checkers

Responsible for initial desk reviews, Bluebook/OSCOLA citation cross-verification, bare act section verification, and editorial formatting.

Double-Blind Peer-Review Process

To guarantee fair, objective, and unbiased evaluation, all research papers and legal articles submitted to Legal Lounge Live undergo a rigorous 4-stage review:

  1. Initial Desk Screening (2–3 Days): Evaluation of scope, originality, relevance to contemporary legal discourse, and plagiarism check (threshold must be under 10% excluding citations).
  2. Double-Blind Peer Review (7–10 Days): The author’s identity is anonymized. Two independent reviewers evaluate the manuscript on legal rigor, research depth, statutory reasoning, and accuracy of citations.
  3. Author Revision Stage: If accepted with revisions, the author is provided with itemized reviewer remarks to refine their legal arguments within 7 days.
  4. Final Proofing & Archival: Assigned persistent permalink, categorized under OSCOLA/Bluebook citation index, and archived in the public legal database.

Publication Ethics & Malpractice Statement (COPE Aligned)

  • Originality & Plagiarism: Authors must submit only 100% original, unpublished work. Republication without express credit or cross-citation is strictly barred.
  • AI Attribution & Transparency: While generative AI may be consulted for exploratory research, all analytical arguments, case commentary, and legal deductions must be authored by the submitter. Generative assistance must be disclosed.
  • Conflict of Interest: Authors must disclose any commercial, financial, or personal interest in the subject matter or litigation discussed in their papers.
  • Corrections & Retractions: Minor typographical errors are updated with timestamped footnotes; substantive errors undergo formal retraction or published correction as per our Corrections Policy.
