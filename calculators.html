Sec 138 NI Act Cheque Bounce Timeline Tracker

The Negotiable Instruments Act imposes strict statutory deadlines. A missed day can cause the complaint to be barred by limitation.

Statutory Limitation Milestones

Step 1: Bank Dishonour (Day 0)

Cheque is dishonoured with remarks like "Funds Insufficient" or "Signature Differs".

Step 2: Statutory Demand Notice (Within 30 Days)

Legal notice must be dispatched to the drawer within 30 days of receiving return memo.

Step 3: 15-Day Cure Window

Drawer has 15 statutory days from date of receipt of notice to make full payment.

Step 4: Criminal Complaint Filing (Within 30 Days)

If unpaid, Section 138 complaint must be filed before the competent Magistrate within 1 month.
