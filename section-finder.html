How to use this table. The Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023, the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023 and the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam, 2023 came into force on 1 July 2024. The old Acts generally still apply to offences committed and proceedings begun before that date, subject to the repeal and savings provisions. A new number does not always mean identical wording, and some old numbers now mean something else (for example, Section 482 of the CrPC and Section 482 of the BNSS).
