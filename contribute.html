Write for Legal Lounge Live

Share your work with other law students, and build a portfolio.

← Back to home

We welcome guest articles and case notes from law students and early-career lawyers. It is a good way to build a public portfolio.

What we are looking for

  • Clear explainers (about 800 to 1,200 words) on a legal topic, written for students.
  • Case notes in our four-part format: facts, issue, reasoning, takeaway.
  • Study guides, mnemonics and exam tips that you have tested yourself.

Our standards

  • Your work must be original and your own. Do not copy from books, judgments, notes or websites.
  • Cite the Constitution, statutes and cases you rely on, with correct citations.
  • Use the current law, including the BNS, BNSS and BSA, and say when the position is unsettled.
  • No legal advice on a live matter, and no confidential or client information.

How it works

  1. Complete the submission form below with your details and a clear summary of your work.
  2. Your submission is saved for review and is not published automatically.
  3. We review the sources and suitability, and may contact you about edits or publication.
  4. If accepted, we agree on author credit and a publishing date.
