Legal Lounge Live welcomes thoughtful, original contributions from law students and early-career professionals. Send us a legal article, case note, or research paper for editorial review. For jobs and internships, use our separate Careers & Internships Portal.

01

What we welcome

Legal explainers, case notes, research papers, and study guides. Submit job and internship listings through the Careers & Internships Portal.

02

Before you submit

Use original work, cite authorities accurately, and check that your submission reflects current law, including the BNS, BNSS and BSA where relevant.

03

What happens next

Every submission is reviewed. Sending work does not guarantee acceptance or publication; we may contact you about edits and author details.

Protect confidentiality. Do not include client information, privileged material, personal data you are not authorised to share, or confidential documents from a live matter.
