How we handle mistakes, and a public log of what we have fixed.

We try to keep every page accurate and current. When we find or are told about a mistake, we fix it and record it here. This page shows our commitment to accuracy and keeps our changes visible.

How to report an error

Send us the page, what you think is wrong, and a reliable source if you have one, through our contact page or LinkedIn.

Our approach

  • Substantive errors are corrected and logged with a date.
  • Typing and formatting fixes are made without a note.
  • Articles show a “last updated” date.
  • Laws change. If a law has been amended or a case overruled, we update the page or note the change.
