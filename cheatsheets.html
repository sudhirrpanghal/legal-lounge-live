The five writs

Supreme Court: Article 32 (Fundamental Rights). High Courts: Article 226 (Fundamental Rights and any other purpose).

WritMeaningUsed when
Habeas corpusYou may have the bodyIllegal detention
MandamusWe commandPublic authority fails a public duty
ProhibitionForbidsLower court or tribunal acting without jurisdiction (ongoing)
CertiorariTo be certifiedQuash an order made without jurisdiction or with a clear legal error
