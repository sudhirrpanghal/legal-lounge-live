Student guides to the BNS, BNSS and BSA, which came into force on 1 July 2024.

BNS

Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023

20 chapters and 358 sections. Replaces the Indian Penal Code: offences and punishments.

Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, 2023

39 chapters and 531 sections. Replaces the CrPC: arrest, FIR, trial, bail and appeals.

12 chapters and 170 sections. Replaces the Evidence Act: what evidence is allowed and how to prove it.

Which law applies? The new codes apply to offences committed on or after 1 July 2024, and the old Acts generally continue for earlier offences and proceedings, subject to the savings provisions.
