Legal maxims, A to Z

26 common Latin maxims with their meanings and where they appear.

← Back to home
Actus non facit reum nisi mens sit reaCriminal law＋

Meaning: An act does not make a person guilty unless the mind is also guilty.
Idea: Most offences need a guilty act and a guilty mind.

Audi alteram partemNatural justice＋

Meaning: Hear the other side.
Idea: A person must get a fair chance to be heard before an adverse order is made.

Caveat emptorContract law＋

Meaning: Let the buyer beware.
Idea: A buyer must inspect goods, subject to statutory protections.

De minimis non curat lexGeneral＋

Meaning: The law does not concern itself with trifles.
Idea: A very small harm may not justify legal action.

Damnum sine injuriaTort law＋

Meaning: Damage without a legal wrong.
Idea: Loss from lawful competition gives no claim.

Ejusdem generisInterpretation＋

Meaning: Of the same kind.
Idea: General words after a list are read as limited to things of the same kind.

Expressio unius est exclusio alteriusInterpretation＋

Meaning: The mention of one thing excludes others.
Idea: If a statute lists specific items, others may be left out.

Ignorantia juris non excusatGeneral＋

Meaning: Ignorance of the law is no excuse.
Idea: A person cannot avoid liability by saying they did not know the law.

Injuria sine damnoTort law＋

Meaning: A legal wrong without actual loss.
Idea: A violation of a legal right can be actionable even without proof of loss.

Nemo dat quod non habetProperty and sale＋

Meaning: No one gives what they do not have.
Idea: A seller generally cannot pass a better title than their own.

Nemo debet bis vexari pro una et eadem causaCriminal law＋

Meaning: No one should be troubled twice for the same cause.
Idea: The idea behind double jeopardy, protected by Article 20(2).

Nemo judex in causa suaNatural justice＋

Meaning: No one should be a judge in their own cause.
Idea: The rule against bias.

Noscitur a sociisInterpretation＋

Meaning: A word is known by the company it keeps.
Idea: The meaning of an unclear word is taken from the words around it.

Obiter dictaPrecedent＋

Meaning: Things said in passing.
Idea: Remarks in a judgment that are not necessary for the decision. They are persuasive, not binding.

Pacta sunt servandaContract law＋

Meaning: Agreements must be kept.
Idea: The basic principle that promises should be honoured.

Per incuriamPrecedent＋

Meaning: Through lack of care.
Idea: A decision made without noticing a binding authority or statute may lose its precedent value.

Ratio decidendiPrecedent＋

Meaning: The reason for the decision.
Idea: The legal principle on which a decision rests.

Res ipsa loquiturTort law＋

Meaning: The thing speaks for itself.
Idea: In some negligence cases the accident itself suggests negligence.

Res judicataCivil procedure＋

Meaning: A matter already judged.
Idea: A matter finally decided between the same parties cannot be re-opened.

Respondeat superiorTort law＋

Meaning: Let the superior answer.
Idea: The basis of vicarious liability of an employer for an employee.

Stare decisisPrecedent＋

Meaning: To stand by what has been decided.
Idea: Courts follow earlier decisions. Under Article 141, Supreme Court law binds all courts.

Sub judiceProcedure＋

Meaning: Under judicial consideration.
Idea: A matter pending before a court.

Ubi jus ibi remediumGeneral＋

Meaning: Where there is a right, there is a remedy.
Idea: A legal right should have a legal remedy.

Ultra viresAdministrative law＋

Meaning: Beyond the powers.
Idea: An act beyond the authority given by law can be challenged.

Vigilantibus non dormientibus jura subveniuntCivil law＋

Meaning: The law helps those who are vigilant, not those who sleep on their rights.
Idea: The idea behind limitation periods.

Volenti non fit injuriaTort law＋

Meaning: No wrong is done to one who consents.
Idea: Consent to a risk can be a defence in tort, subject to limits.
