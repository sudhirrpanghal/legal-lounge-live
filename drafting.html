CRIMINAL PROCEDURE

Regular Bail Application under Section 483 BNSS, 2023

Filed before Court of Sessions / High Court (formerly Section 439 CrPC).

Litigation Practice Note: Section 483 BNSS replaces Section 439 CrPC. Ensure all grounds specify why custody is no longer required for investigation and that the accused has roots in society.
